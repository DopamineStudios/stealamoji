# Steal a 'Moji Discord Bot - Privacy Policy

<sub>Last updated: 22 November, 2026</sub>

## 1. Introduction

Dopamine Studios (“**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) operates **Steal a 'Moji** (also known as Stealamoji), a free Discord bot for copying custom emojis into your server. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use Stealamoji.

By using Steal a 'Moji, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

---

## 2. Information We Process

Steal a 'Moji is intentionally small. We process only what is needed to run `/steal` and related features:

### 2.1 From Discord

- your **Discord user ID**, username / display name (as provided by Discord when you run a command);
- the **server (guild) ID** where the command is used;
- the **emoji** you provide (emoji ID, name, and image downloaded from Discord’s CDN); and
- **Custom Emojis across servers**: The bot automatically scans and caches **ALL** custom emojis (names, IDs, animated status, and associated server names) from all servers it joins, maintaining an in-memory index to power interactive autocomplete suggestions for the `/steal` command. Cleared automatically when you remove the bot from the server.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, or postal address.

### 2.2 Operational data

We may keep limited operational logs (for example, errors) and uptime health checks so the bot can stay online. These are for reliability.

### 2.3 What we do not sell

We **do not sell** your personal information.
